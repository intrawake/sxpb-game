Don't Mess with Cthulhu is a social deduction game.
Roles are hidden: Investigators want to find all the Elder Signs. Cultists want to find Cthulhu or stall until the end of Round 4.
Players shuffle their hands and deal them face down in front of them.
The player with the flashlight chooses exactly one card in front of another player to reveal.
If it's an Elder Sign, the Investigators are one step closer to winning.
If it's Cthulhu, the Cultists win immediately!
If it's a Blank, nothing happens.
After a card is revealed, the person whose card was revealed takes the flashlight.
Before using the flashlight, players should talk to the table, ask questions, or claim what cards they have in front of them (truthfully or bluffing).
