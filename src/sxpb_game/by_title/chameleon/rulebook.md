Chameleon is a social deduction word game.
The GM picks 16 words, and one of them is the secret word.
One player is the Leader, and one player is the Chameleon.
Everyone except the Chameleon knows the secret word.
The Leader says a word related to the secret word, followed by the rest of the players.
The Chameleon must blend in and say a word without knowing the secret word.
Then, everyone discusses for 2 rounds and votes on who the Chameleon is.
The Leader does not vote, but breaks ties.
If the Chameleon escapes the vote, they win. If caught, they can still win by guessing the secret word.
