Connect Four is a game between 2 players with red (R) and yellow (Y) discs respectively.
The goal is to be the first to connect 4 of your discs in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.
The board is a grid of 7 columns and 6 rows.
Columns are labeled with letters from left to right.
Rows are labeled with numbers from bottom to top.

Gravity forces discs to fall to the lowest-numbered row that isn't already occupied.
Therefore, for every column on the board with an open space, there is exactly 1 valid location to place a disc.
