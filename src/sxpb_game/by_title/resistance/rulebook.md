# The Resistance

A social deduction game of secret identities, deduction, and deception.

Players are either **Resistance** operatives or Imperial **Spies**. The game lasts up to 5 rounds. Each round has a Leader who proposes a squad to go on a mission.

## Phases
1. **PROPOSE_SQUAD**: The current Leader proposes a squad.
2. **VOTE_ON_SQUAD**: All players vote to approve or reject the squad.
   - If a majority approves, the squad goes on the mission.
   - If rejected (or tied), the vote track advances and a **DISCUSSION** phase begins before the next leader proposes a squad.
   - **Crucial:** If 5 squads are rejected in a single round, the Spies win the game immediately!
3. **MISSION_VOTE**: Players on the squad secretly play a 'success' or 'sabotage' card.
   - The mission fails if there is at least 1 sabotage (or 2 sabotages for Mission 4 in games with 7+ players).
   - Otherwise, the mission succeeds.
   - Resistance players MUST play success. Spies can play success or sabotage.
4. **DISCUSSION**: After a squad is rejected or a mission is resolved, players take turns discussing the results before the next leader proposes a squad.

## Winning
- **Resistance** wins if 3 missions succeed.
- **Spies** win if 3 missions fail, OR if the vote track reaches 5.
