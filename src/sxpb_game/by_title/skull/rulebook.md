Skull is a pure bluffing game.

Every player starts with 4 discs: 3 Roses and 1 Skull.
On your turn, you can either play a disc face-down on your pile, OR if you feel brave (and everyone has played at least one disc), you can start the bidding.

- `play rose <optional banter>`: Play a rose from your hand face down.
- `play skull <optional banter>`: Play your skull face down.
- `bid <number> <optional banter>`: Start bidding or raise the bid.
- `pass <optional banter>`: Pass during bidding.

Bidding means declaring how many discs you think you can flip over across the whole table *without* hitting a skull.
The highest bidder becomes the Challenger.

The Challenger MUST flip all of their *own* played discs first! Then they can pick from the top of other players' piles.
- `flip my stack <optional banter>`: Flip your own stack's top disc.
- `flip p<idx> <optional banter>`: Flip the top disc of another player's stack.
- `p<idx>? <banter>`: Hover over another player's stack to mindgame them before flipping.

- Hit a skull: Lose the challenge and lose one of your discs permanently.
- Flip only roses: Win the challenge! Win two challenges to win the whole game.
