Minesweeper is a grid puzzle game where the objective is to clear a board containing hidden mines without detonating any of them.
Numbers on revealed squares indicate how many mines are adjacent to that square (including diagonally).
You can provide multiple moves in a single turn by separating them with spaces (e.g., 'Oa1 Fb2 ?c3').
Valid actions for a coordinate (e.g., 'a1'):
- 'Oa1': Open (reveal) the square at a1.
- 'Fa1': Place a flag on a1 to mark a suspected mine.
- '?a1': Place a question mark on a1.
- 'Ua1': Remove a flag or question mark from the square at a1.
