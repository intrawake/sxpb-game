Welcome to Twenty Questions! This is a classic game of deduction.

There are two players: the **Oracle** and the **Guesser**.
1. The Oracle begins by choosing a secret word.
2. The Guesser may ask up to 20 yes/no questions to figure out the secret word. Refer to the secret word as "it" to keep questions quick (e.g., "Is it man-made?", "Is it blue?").
3. The Oracle must answer each question honestly with 'yes', 'no', 'somewhat', 'usually', 'rarely', or 'idk' (I don't know).
4. If the Oracle answers 'idk', that question does not count toward the 20-question limit.
5. To guess the word, the Guesser simply includes the word at the end of their question or statement. The game automatically detects if the secret word is the last word in the Guesser's input (ignoring punctuation).
6. If the Guesser guesses the secret word within 20 counted questions, the Guesser wins!
7. If the Guesser asks 20 counted questions without guessing the word, the Oracle wins.
